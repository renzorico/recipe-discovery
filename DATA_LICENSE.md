# Data

The catalogue in `data/recipes.parquet` is derived from a public dataset of
Food.com recipes:

<https://huggingface.co/datasets/Karo8870/food.com-parsed-dataset>

Recipe content is © its original authors on Food.com. It is provided here
solely for the purposes of this technical assessment. Please do not
redistribute it.

The snapshot has been filtered and sampled from the full catalogue, and
the contributor username column has been removed.
